## 🎙️ Listen Closely. Stay Safe.

**Voice Phishing Detector** is a cutting-edge, AI-powered security tool designed to protect you from the fastest-growing cyber threat: **Vishing (Voice Phishing)**. 

Scammers are increasingly using AI-generated voices (deepfakes) and social engineering tactics to trick people into revealing sensitive information over the phone. This tool acts as your personal audio firewall. By analyzing the acoustic properties, speech patterns, and known scammer scripts in real-time, it alerts you when a call sounds suspicious.

Whether it's a fake bank representative, a "tech support" agent, or a cloned voice of a family member, the detector uses advanced machine learning models to calculate a **Threat Score** and warns you before you make a mistake.

> 💡 **Why use this detector?** Because you can't always trust your ears anymore. Deepfakes are getting too good. Let the AI listen for the subtle artifacts and aggressive urgency tactics that human ears miss.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. Protect your device.**

### 1️⃣ Download the application
Grab the latest binary for Windows/macOS or the APK for Android from the button at the top.

### 2️⃣ Grant microphone permissions
The app needs access to your call audio to analyze it locally. **No audio is ever recorded or stored.**

### 3️⃣ Start the background service
Toggle the protection switch ON. The AI will now listen for threats during your calls.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Are my calls being recorded?</b></summary>

**No.** The audio is processed in real-time in your device's RAM. It is immediately discarded after the analysis. We have no servers, and nothing is uploaded.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can it detect normal human liars?</b></summary>

It's not a lie detector. It focuses on detecting **synthetic audio** (deepfakes) and **known social engineering patterns** (like asking for gift cards or remote access).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work on WhatsApp and Telegram calls?</b></summary>

Yes, if you are using the Android or Desktop version, it captures the system audio loopback, meaning it can analyze VoIP calls as well as standard cellular calls.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How often are the scam scripts updated?</b></summary>

The NLP engine connects to a community-driven database of known scam scripts. It updates automatically every 24 hours to include new tactics.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include your OS, device model, and a screenshot of the error log.
</details>
